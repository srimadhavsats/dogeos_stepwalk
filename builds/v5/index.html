Clickable prototype of the DogeOS move-to-earn dapp. Everything is fake data, running in your browser. Open this link on a phone for the full-screen app.

  1. Pick a faction in onboarding (or on Home → the Heist bar): Demolition Dogs, Kernel Crew or Barkitects.
  2. Home: faction missions and the weekly signal standings. Tap 🐾 +1,000 steps to push your faction up.
  3. Battle: break into the Fat Cat Vault (10 floors, Fat Cat boss on floor 10). Watch out for squirrels.
  4. Cards: every dog now shows its faction stripe and role. Train, fuse, gear up or mint.
  5. Wild: befriend the dogs on your trail to grow your crew.
