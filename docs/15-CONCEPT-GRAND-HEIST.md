# 15 · Concept D: built around the DogeOS Grand Heist (build v5)

> **Status: concept under test** (prototype build v5). It keeps everything from Pack Battles (14) and regroups it around the official **DogeOS Grand Heist** lore, so DogeOS users recognise it instantly.
> **IP note:** the Grand Heist, its faction names, lore and art belong to DogeOS / Layer 69 Foundation. v5 uses only the names and the premise, with our own art and a visible "not affiliated" notice. **Get written permission before any public launch** (see §6). If permission is refused, switch back to build v4.

## 1. What the Grand Heist is (from the official site)
- **What:** the Grand Heist is DogeOS's community questing campaign. Users complete quests across ecosystem apps ("crews") and earn collectible loot. It is reachable from the MyDoge wallet.
  - Sources: [heist.dogeos.com](https://heist.dogeos.com) and [quest.dogeos.com](https://quest.dogeos.com).
- **Premise:** the value Dogecoin created has flowed to the **Fat Cats** who run the exchanges. The Heist moves it back on-chain by getting people to use doge-native apps.
  - Tagline: *"Take Back What Is Yours."*
  - Their stated aim is not to destroy the Fat Cats but to remove the community's dependence on them.
- **Squirrels:** anything that distracts from the mission, such as price FUD, drama and shiny new coins. The standing order is *ignore the squirrels*.
- **Crews:** the protocol teams building the apps that quests use. About 29 crews are listed today, including BarkSwap, Dappit, Doge Museum, DogeFood Lab, MuchFi, Neova, Laika and Starstone, and the site says more join every day.
- **Timeline:**
  - A Galxe pre-op to "assemble the crew" ran from around late May 2026.
  - The full Heist is live now (October 2026).

## 2. How v5 maps our game onto the Heist
**Factions replace our 6 classes.** Each faction holds two battle roles, so all 18 breeds and the battle variety from v4 stay the same.
| Faction | Role (was) | Breeds | Special move |
|---|---|---|---|
| Demolition Dogs | 🧨 Breacher (Guardian) | Doberman, German Shepherd, Rottweiler | **Door Breach**: shield 30% HP, holds aggro 2 rounds |
| Demolition Dogs | 📡 Runner (Sprinter) | Greyhound, Whippet, Border Collie | **Signal Burst**: two quick strikes |
| Kernel Crew | 📣 Amplifier (Charmer) | Labrador, Golden Retriever, Corgi | **Viral Thread**: distracts the strongest enemy, heals the weakest ally |
| Kernel Crew | 🗂️ Intel (Tracker) | Beagle, Bloodhound, Dachshund | **Exploit Found**: 1.8× hit on the weakest enemy |
| Barkitects | 🛠️ Engineer (Trekker) | Husky, Malamute, Saint Bernard | **Hotfix**: heals 35% HP |
| Barkitects | 💻 Operator (Legend) | Shiba Inu, Akita, Samoyed | **Ship It**: team +15% ATK, then a hit |

**Faction traits become game effects** (the official power and weakness, turned into gameplay):
| Faction | Super power in battle | Weakness in battle |
|---|---|---|
| Demolition Dogs | *Always Online*: +20% SPD and +10% ATK in round 1 | *No Wi-Fi*: −25% SPD on Dead Zone floors |
| Kernel Crew | *Viral Bark*: specials charge 40% faster | *Squirrels*: twice as likely to chase one |
| Barkitects | *Utility Maxxing*: gear bonuses ×1.5 | *Full Degen*: under 30% HP, deal **and** take +25% damage |

**Other new rules:**
- **Matchups:** Demolition Dogs beat Barkitects (they smash builds before they ship), Barkitects beat Kernel Crew (they ship before the thread is finished), and Kernel Crew beat Demolition Dogs (the plan beats the noise). The winner of a matchup does ×1.3 damage.
- **Crew bonus:** 3 or more dogs from one faction on a team get +10% ATK.
- **Squirrel events:** each round has a 12% chance that a squirrel appears and a random dog loses its turn chasing it.
- **Fat Cat Vault (replaces the Challenge Tower):** 10 floors of Fat Cat security, played as a heist with no violence.
  - Enemies: Doorman, Auditor, Trader, Lawyer and Banker cats. On floor 10, **the Fat Cat** himself (top hat and monocle).
  - Floor rules: Dead Zone (3), Security Door (5), Squirrel Storm (6), Audit floor (9), Boss (10).
  - Gold Challenge cards are still on floors 5 and 10.
- **Faction missions** mirror the official faction reward tracks:
  - Demolition Dogs: share a recap, recruit a Shibe
  - Kernel Crew: post a tip, cheer three walkers
  - Barkitects: claim your treats, fuse or mint a card

  Each faction also has a 6,000-step mission. Faction XP raises your faction rank: Recruit → Operative → Specialist → Mastermind.
- **Signal standings:** a weekly faction-vs-faction race measured in total steps. The winning faction's walkers get a Vault key.
- **Wording:**
  - Season Pass → **Heist Pass** ("Take back what is yours").
  - A beaten dog is **tuckered out** (it naps), never "KO".
  - Every page shows the not-affiliated notice.

**Unchanged from v4:**
- card tiers, levels, fusion, gear, support cards
- Bark Points
- league caps
- the shop and its rules
- Wild encounters
- walking as the grind
- tokenomics (03)

## 3. Why this is the right move
- DogeOS users who already picked a faction in the official Heist can use the same identity here, with no new story to learn.
- **Walking fits the Heist's goal.** Our app is another doge-native tool people actually use, which is exactly what the Heist rewards. Every walk can count as a mission.
- The faction-vs-faction step race gives the community something to rally around every week.

## 4. Lore impact
- The "Wow / Gloom" lore draft in the shared doc is **on hold** while v5 is tested. In v5 the Fat Cats and the Squirrels are the antagonists.
- Our own lore stays inside that frame: why each dog joined its faction, and the stories of the six Diamond legends as famous heist operatives.

## 5. Risks
- **Permission and IP:** the faction names, the premise and "Take Back What Is Yours" are DogeOS / Layer 69 Foundation's. Using them publicly without consent could cause friction with the very partner we want. We don't copy their art, and we keep the notice.
- **Timing:** the Heist may run in seasons. If it ends or changes, our factions need an update path (feature flag + a season reset).
- **Tone:** heist language stays playful: no violence and no real-world exchange names. The Fat Cats are cartoon cats in suits.

## 6. Partnership path (recommended next step)
