Clickable prototype of the DogeOS move-to-earn dapp. Everything is fake data, running in your browser. Open this link on a phone for the full-screen app.

  1. Tap 🐾 +1,000 steps twice for the goal confetti and a Daily Box. Tap once more to level up.
  2. Wild (concept test): meet the dogs waiting on your trail, befriend them with the trust meter, fill the Pawdex, then battle in the Arena.
  3. Den → Loot: open your boxes, then run tonight's Genesis Draw.
  4. Den → Closet: dress your Walker (try the wheelchair base). Market: buy, sell, trade or rent Genesis NFTs.
  5. Play: run Fetch Frenzy (arrow keys or tap) and watch a Doge Derby race.
  6. Walk: start a run, flip Go Live and turn on 🔊. Ask Coach "Should I buy more TREAT?"
